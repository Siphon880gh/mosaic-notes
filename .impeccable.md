## Design Context

### Users
Students, nurses, and people at work who build a one-page template, print it, and fill it in with a pen. The job is a letter-size sheet of boxes: instructions and pictures go in on screen, handwriting happens on paper. Nursing brainsheets are the reference use. They are often in a hurry, on a desktop, and they return to the same sheet.

### Brand Personality
Paper, quiet, useful. The voice is a person at a desk, not a product launch. Say "sheet", "card", and "print". Short labels. No cheerleading. The feeling to leave is that the page is ready for a pen.

### Aesthetic Direction
A sheet of paper on a gray desk. The page is the product: black rules, readable type, letter size. Chrome sits at the edge and disappears when printed. Ink is warm near-black (`#1c1915`), paper is warm off-white (`#f7f4ee`), and the one action color is the green already used for hover (`#166534`). Tool labels use a serif (Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Georgia) so they read as desk labels. Text inside the cards stays in the browser face, because that is what prints.

Anti-references: a dashboard, a dark IDE, glassmorphism, gradient headlines, and a command line as the way to open your own work.

### Design Principles
1. The sheet is the product. Controls stay off the page and hide in print.
2. The row shows four things: Open, Print, More, and New text card. Write, cards, layout, and style open one group at a time inside More.
3. Progressive disclosure. Borders, spacing, opacity, italics, duplicate, delete, and the command palette wait until asked.
4. Files are recognized, not remembered. Opening a sheet is a list of saved names, never a typed command.
5. A tour may reset the canvas only after the person agrees. Popovers stay inside the window, show the step index and Previous, and sit above every other layer.

### Visual system
- Primary button: green fill `#166534`, paper text `#f7f4ee`, 40px tall.
- Quiet button: transparent, ink text, 1px rule.
- More: 40px icon button immediately beside New text card.
- Dialogs and tour popovers: paper fill, ink rule, 16px body, 22px title. Keep 12px inside the viewport. Scroll the contents rather than clipping them.
- Stacking, low to high: icon rail 91, toolbar 92, command palette 9999, file list 10020, tour dim 10040, highlighted target 10055, tour popover 10070, reset confirmation 10080.
